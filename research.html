Research by Theme

My research connects trustworthy AI, AI for security, search and retrieval, multimedia, privacy, and content protection. This page groups selected work by theme; the complete record is on the publications and patents pages.

Trustworthy AI & AI Security

Making AI systems more trustworthy and robust: defending models and agents, detecting and removing backdoors, protecting model integrity and intellectual property, and improving adversarial robustness.

  • PVDI: Preserving Vital and Disrupting Irrelevant Latent Attentions for Robust Backdoor Defense IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security, 2026 A latent-attention approach that neutralizes hidden backdoor triggers while preserving the model's useful behavior.
  • ABDP: Adversarial Backdoor Detection and Purification IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security, 2026 Detects whether a trained model carries hidden backdoors and purifies poisoned training data to produce a backdoor-free model.
  • BehaviorGuard — account-compromise detection for human-operated ransomware Microsoft Defender for Endpoint · technology transfer Multi-resolution, multi-stage user-behavior modeling that catches compromised accounts during lateral movement, the top enterprise threat; first stage shipped.
  • Hardening malicious-PowerShell detection Microsoft Defender for Endpoint · technology transfer Built a strong evasion attack against the production detector, then a two-classifier defense that markedly improved robust accuracy under attack; countermeasures were adopted.

AI Assistant Quality, Evaluation & Prompt Optimization

Improving AI assistants through rigorous evaluation, replay infrastructure, model selection, agentic deep search, and automatic prompt optimization.

  • Automatic and strategic-guided prompt optimization (AMPO / StraGo) EMNLP 2024 · deployed across multiple Bing teams Methods that automatically search and refine prompts, including tree-structured and strategy-guided optimization, adopted by several product teams.
  • Evaluating LLM-Based Agents for Multi-Turn Conversations: A Survey ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology, 2026 A systematic framing of how to measure multi-turn agent quality — the foundation for trustworthy assistant evaluation.
  • Reasoning-quality evaluation and model selection for M365 Copilot Microsoft 365 Copilot · applied research Evaluation and replay infrastructure plus model-switching that improved measured citation and answer quality in production-facing settings.

Search Quality & Neural Retrieval

Better retrieval at web scale, from multi-vector neural retrieval and document understanding to index compression with large cost savings and no speed sacrifice.

  • Multi-vector retrieval with XTR + ColBERT Bing Core Search · applied research A multi-vector retrieval approach that outperformed dense retrieval and approached production sparse+dense quality.
  • Bing reverse-index (PDI) compression Bing · technology transfer Roughly 23% average storage savings with no loss in compression/decompression speed — estimated to save at least $10M per year, and shipped.
  • Structure-based adaptive document caching US Patent 9,195,773 Caching that exploits document structure to improve web serving performance.

Human Interactive Proofs (CAPTCHA) & Authentication

Distinguishing humans from bots and legitimate users from impostors, with a broad CAPTCHA portfolio, authentication research, and security analyses of graphical passwords.

  • Visual HIP and Audio HIP transferred to Windows Live Technology transfer · in production use since ~2010 Production human-interactive-proof technology deployed after the prior CAPTCHA was broken.
  • Breaking password-discretization for click-based graphical passwords WWW 2013, ACM CCS 2014, IEEE TIFS Proved that all proposed discretization schemes leak password information and are vulnerable to dictionary attacks, overturning prior assumptions.
  • Image-based CAPTCHA exploiting object-recognition context US Patent 9,317,676 (among a broad HIP patent portfolio) One of several patented designs spanning manipulable, overlay, and audio HIPs and human-computation frameworks.
  • Zero-effort smartphone locking with wristwear & device-assisted authentication Research & concept work The first zero-effort scheme to lock/unlock phones via wristwear, plus a secure-mouse concept anticipating later hardware-token and passkey patterns.

Phishing, Fraud & Account Protection

Protecting users and platforms from abuse through machine learning, content analysis, evasion-resistant logo recognition, fraud detection, and privacy-constrained defenses.

  • Anti-phishing for Office 365 email Technology transfer (OCR logo recognizer shipped Nov. 2017) Webpage classification, content-based invariant features, and OCR/DNN logo detection engineered to be hard to evade.
  • Phishing defense for Microsoft Forms under strict privacy constraints Technology transfer Tiered reputation, sensitive-input detection, warp-robust logo detection (~99% accuracy), and FXAM, a fast, explainable anti-phishing model that cut review load.
  • Malicious URL detection & account-based fraud classification US Patents 9,178,901 and 9,189,746 Detection of malicious URLs and machine-learning classification of accounts for credit-card-fraud defense in Microsoft payment scenarios.

Multimedia Security, Watermarking & Content Protection

A long-running line of work on protecting media: audio, image, and video watermarking; scalable encryption and DRM; and data embedding for images, charts, and slides.

  • Cognicity watermarking products (AudioKey, tMark, TurnKey) Cognicity, Inc. · acquired by Digimarc, 2001 Commercial audio/image/video watermarking, including transactional download-time watermarking and per-CD unique IDs; contributions to MUSE, the 4C DVD-Audio proposal, and SDMI screening.
  • Scalable media encryption & DRM for MPEG-4 and JPEG2000 Book chapters (2006) & an extensive patent portfolio Syntax-compliant, fully scalable encryption and layered access control that preserve scalability and enable transcoding without decryption.
  • Chartem & ChartStamp — recovering data locked in chart images IEEE VIS 2020 & ACM Multimedia 2022 The first data-embedding approach to reconstruct chart objects from images (enabling "Speak Charts" for visually impaired users), and a DNN encoder–decoder robust to printing, recapture, and JPEG.

Privacy-Preserving Computation & Systems Reliability

Computing on protected data and keeping large systems healthy: processing in the homomorphic-encryption domain, analytics on encrypted video, and unsupervised anomaly detection and outage prediction for cloud services.

  • Decompression in the homomorphic-encryption domain ACM Multimedia 2018 (JPEG) & ICASSP 2019 (FLAC) The first JPEG decompression realized in the HE domain, and HE-domain FLAC decoding that generalizes to several lossless audio formats.
  • Privacy-preserving motion detection on encrypted, HEVC-compressed video ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing, Communications, and Applications, 2022 Motion detection and trajectory inference directly on encrypted surveillance video, with detection comparable to the unencrypted case.
  • Cloud outage prediction & self-cleansing anomaly detection for Azure Applied research Topology- and ML-based incident correlation reaching high precision/recall for major tenants, plus an unsupervised, label-free anomaly detector for storage logs.
