How I Mentor

My role is hands-on and continuous. I help students develop research judgment, evaluate ideas rigorously, and turn early intuition into clear, publishable work.

  • Problem selection and framing. Identify high-impact, tractable problems in trustworthy AI and security, sharpen the research question, and define the contribution clearly.
  • Literature and gap analysis. Read and discuss related work with students so they understand what is known, what is missing, and why the proposed direction matters.
  • Method design. Shape the technical approach, threat model, assumptions, system design, and evaluation plan, while comparing alternative paths before committing.
  • Experiments and evidence. Advise on datasets, baselines, metrics, ablations, and fair comparisons, then review results carefully to distinguish real progress from artifacts.
  • Iteration and problem solving. Meet regularly to diagnose failures, unblock implementation and experiments, refine the idea, and keep the project moving.
  • Paper writing. Coach the whole writing process: story, structure, contribution framing, figures and tables, technical precision, and repeated revision until the paper is clear.
  • Submission and rebuttal. Help choose suitable venues, prepare submissions, write rebuttals, and revise the work after reviews.
  • Career development. Support students as they grow into independent researchers, including presentation skills, scientific rigor, research judgment, and career planning for academia or industry.
