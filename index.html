Broad AI research and trustworthy AI.

I am a Professor at the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA). At BIMSA, I am expanding my research toward broader questions in AI — how AI systems reason, interact with people, and support real-world problem solving — while continuing my work on trustworthy AI and AI security. Recent collaborations also include work with Microsoft Research Asia and Princeton University on societal AI, including AI-assisted education.

I welcome motivated students at all levels — undergraduate, master's, and Ph.D. — as well as postdoctoral researchers interested in broad AI research, trustworthy AI, AI-assisted systems, and AI security.

I also co-supervise student research, including collaborations with Huazhong University of Science and Technology since 2018 and Shanghai Jiao Tong University since 2025. Four Ph.D. students I co-supervised have graduated; three became assistant professors at Chinese universities, including one at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and one joined a major Chinese technology company as a researcher.

Before BIMSA

Before joining BIMSA, I spent 24+ years at Microsoft — including 22 years at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA) and 3 years in product teams for M365 BizChat / Copilot, the Researcher agent, and Bing Core Search — plus 4 years of startup experience as co-founder and lead scientist of Cognicity, commercializing multimedia watermarking technology. Earlier and parallel work spans privacy-preserving computation, authentication, CAPTCHA, Web systems, multimedia security, and watermarking; a fuller account is in Experience and Publications.
