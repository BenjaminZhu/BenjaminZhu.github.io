Trustworthy AI and AI for security, from deep research to product impact.

For 24+ years at Microsoft — across Microsoft Research Asia and product teams for M365 Copilot and Bing Core Search — my work has followed one line: make AI more trustworthy, use AI to solve security and real-world product problems, and carry research results into systems people use. Today this centers on trustworthy AI, AI-driven security, and the quality of AI assistants and search, grounded in long-running work on security, privacy, multimedia, and content protection.
