Personal

I enjoy getting outdoors, seeing new places, and spending time at a slower, steadier pace.

Hiking

I've been hiking for more than 24 years, and I still enjoy the moment when a trail reaches a ridge and the whole valley opens up below. I like quiet paths, mountain air, small towns along the way, and the simple pleasure of a good meal after a long day outside.

Running

I've also run more than 20 full marathons. I like the energy of race morning, the crowds along the course, the rhythm of running with thousands of people, and the joy of crossing the finish line tired and happy.
