Fall 2026: Database — Principles and Applications

This fall semester, I am teaching Database — Principles and Applications for 北京雁栖湖数学与人工智能学院, a joint program between BIMSA and two universities in Beijing. The course covers the theoretical foundations of database systems together with practical, applied problem-solving. Topics include data modeling, relational algebra and SQL, transaction processing, indexing and query optimization, and how these principles connect to real-world system design.
